## 1. What is Promise.race()?

Promise.race() returns the result of the first Promise that is completed, whether it is fulfilled or rejected.

## 2. What is the WHERE clause in SQL?

The WHERE clause is used to filter rows based on a condition. For example, `WHERE age > 18`.

## 3. What are LIMIT and OFFSET clauses in SQL?

`LIMIT` specifies how many rows to return, while `OFFSET` specifies how many rows to skip before returning the results.

## 4. What is the HAVING clause in SQL?

The HAVING clause is used to filter grouped results after using GROUP BY.

## 5. What is ORM?

ORM stands for Object-Relational Mapping. It allows us to work with database tables using objects and classes instead of writing SQL queries directly.

## 6. What is authentication?

Authentication is the process of checking whether a user is who they claim to be, usually using a username, email, and password.

## 7. What is the difference between `CharField` and `TextField` in Django?

`CharField` is generally used for shorter text with a maximum length. `TextField` is used for larger amounts of text.

## 8. Explain `on_delete=CASCADE`.

`on_delete=CASCADE` means when a related parent record is deleted, its related child records are also deleted automatically.
